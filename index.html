Obsidian Vault Backup

A personal backup tool used by its owner only.

Home Privacy policy Terms of service

What it does

Obsidian Vault Backup is a script that runs on the owner's own computer. It takes regular snapshots of the owner's Obsidian note vaults, encrypts them with restic, keeps one copy on the local disk, and uploads a second copy to the owner's own Google Drive using rclone.

Who uses it

One person: the owner of this site. The tool is not offered to anyone else, has no sign-up, and is not distributed as an app or extension. This page exists because Google requires a public home page and privacy policy for any application that connects to Google Drive, even a private one.

How it connects to Google Drive

  • It uses Google's OAuth 2.0 to get permission from the owner's Google Account, once, on the owner's machine.
  • It only reads and writes inside backup folders in the owner's own Google Drive.
  • Everything it uploads is encrypted before it leaves the computer. Google Drive only ever holds encrypted files.
  • There is no server, website login, database, or third party involved in the backup process.

Source

The tool is built from open-source software: restic for encrypted snapshots and rclone for the Google Drive transfer. The owner's wrapper script and configuration are private and are not published.
